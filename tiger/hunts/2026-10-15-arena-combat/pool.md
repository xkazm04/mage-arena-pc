## P01 - Turn Lab Replay into a Forkable Counterfactual Sandbox with Ghost Diffs

`leap` `effort M` round 1 - anchors 7/7 - peers 5.3/10 (n=3)

- **Outcome:** While tuning combat feel in the Lab, a designer or player can pause at any moment of a 20-second exchange, scrub backward and forward tick-by-tick, drag any feel slider or toggle between presets ('Current', 'Snappier', 'Heavier'), and immediately see a translucent ghost of their original play side-by-side with the counterfactual trajectory re-simulated from their exact human inputs—complete with live A/B metric deltas and a one-click 'Take Over Here' branch or 25 KB deterministic Feel Capsule export.
- **Builds on:** Evolves the deterministic 60 Hz `stepArena` / `stateHash` kernel (`packages/core/src/arena/kernel.ts:275-308`), the 1,200-tick `CombatLab.history` ring buffer (`packages/core/src/arena/lab.ts:52-62`), and the event-sourced `ArenaScene` renderer (`packages/game/src/arena-scene.ts:530-630`) that already re-derives visuals cleanly when `state` is swapped.
- **Mechanism:** 1) In `packages/core/src/arena/lab.ts:52-62,144-212`, store a parallel 1,200-entry `inputTape: InputFrame[]` ring buffer plus the ring-start base `ArenaState` snapshot and `LabTuningSnapshot`. 2) Add a pure `resimulateLab(lab, nextTuning, upToIndex)` helper that clones the ring-start snapshot and runs `stepArena` across `inputTape` (1,200 ticks takes ~2.6 ms CPU), producing a parallel `counterfactualHistory: ArenaState[]` and `counterfactualMetrics: LabMetrics` without mutating the baseline recording. 3) In `packages/game/src/lab-ui.ts:151-166,520-653,814-865`, add `,`/`.` bidirectional tick scrubbing, live counterfactual re-simulation whenever a tuning slider moves while paused/replaying, an A/B metric diff banner (`Perfects: 2 -> 4 | Staggers: 1 -> 2`), and a `Take Over` key (`B`) that truncates the tape at the scrub cursor and resumes live play from the counterfactual state. 4) In `packages/game/src/arena-scene.ts:530-630`, optionally render the baseline snapshot's player/enemy transforms as a `0.35`-alpha wireframe/tinted ghost mesh and projectile trail. 5) Upgrade `exportTuning` (`packages/core/src/arena/tuning.ts:346-385`, `packages/game/src/lab-ui.ts:738-751`) to v4 Feel Capsules bundling `{ config, tuning, inputTape, startSnapshot, expectedStateHash }`.
- **Evidence:** Observed in code: `changeLabTuning` (`packages/core/src/arena/lab.ts:213-223`) only affects future ticks and marks `lab.metrics.mixed = true`, while `LabUI.export` (`packages/game/src/lab-ui.ts:749`) prints `'Include the seed and setup in your feel report'` because `exportTuning` (`packages/core/src/arena/tuning.ts:352-360`) drops the seed, `LabConfig`, and player inputs. Observed in benchmark: 500-bout Monte Carlo sweeps in `packages/core/src/arena/balance.test.ts:19-41` execute ~1.8M ticks in a few seconds (~2.2 us/tick), so re-simulating a 1,200-tick (20s) window on slider drag takes <3 ms. Prior art: Bret Victor's *Inventing on Principle* (https://vimeo.com/36579366) demonstrates how coupling live parameter manipulation to recorded input trajectories transforms game-feel tuning from blind trial-and-error into direct visual inspection.
- **Risk:** Counterfactual re-simulation could diverge if `ArenaState` cloning misses nested arrays (`randomLog`, `events`), or ghost meshes could clutter the arena during 5-unit skirmishes; mitigate by asserting `stateHash(resimulateLab(lab, lab.tuning).at(-1)) === stateHash(lab.state)` in unit tests and rendering ghost overlays only for the player and primary locked target while paused.
- **Verify:** 1) Automated test in `packages/core/src/arena/arena.test.ts`: record 600 ticks of play, call `resimulateLab` with identical tuning, and assert exact `stateHash` equality; call `resimulateLab` with `absorbWindowS: 0.22` vs `0.10` on a tape containing a 9-tick-old ward contact and assert `perfectAbsorbs` flips from `0` to `1`. 2) Performance check: 1,200-tick counterfactual re-sim completes in `< 5 ms` on desktop.

2. Open an entry per threat when it is first seen, keyed by activation id, aimed at the actor.
3. Close the entry from facts the kernel already knows: the hit event's guarded flag and perfect event; age = hit tick minus absorbFreshTick, so early-by = age minus the window in ticks; immune at impact means rolled through (resolveHit returns with no event at kernel.ts:70, so this is invisible today); an unguarded frontal hit followed by a raise within 0.5 s gives late-by k; outside the arc means wrong facing; the threat vanished without contact means avoided; anything else is counted as unclassified.
