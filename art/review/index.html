  1. Forum in Four Inks — weakest 3; mean 3; shortlist input

    The bold, flat color blocking and clean geometry provide the highest legibility; the player, the cyan absorb shield, and the red threats are instantly distinguishable in the arena, while the camp map reads like a clear schematic with distinct zones. Concern: The extreme sparseness and lack of texture may feel too abstract or 'UI-like' for a narrative game, potentially lacking the atmospheric depth of the other options.

  2. Tessera & Lime — weakest 3; mean 3; shortlist input

    The fresco-like texture and warm palette create a cohesive, grounded world; the arena clearly separates the player's blue shield from the red enemies, and the camp map feels like a tangible, lived-in location with clear landmarks. Concern: The hand-painted texture can sometimes muddy the fine details of the characters, making them slightly less distinct than in the cleaner styles.

  3. Hearth Under Guard — weakest 3; mean 3; shortlist input

    The soft, rounded shapes and pastel palette make the game feel approachable and clear; the arena action is easy to follow, and the camp map is charming and legible without being cluttered. Concern: The soft edges and low contrast might make it harder to distinguish specific UI elements or critical game states (like damage or status effects) at a glance.

  4. Ember After Dark — weakest 3; mean 3; other direction

    The high-contrast lighting creates a dramatic, focused view of the action; the glowing cyan shield and orange spells pop against the dark background, making the core mechanics very clear. Concern: The dark, heavy shadows in the camp map can obscure details and make the environment feel less navigable or inviting compared to the brighter options.

  5. Ash & Bronze — weakest 3; mean 3; other direction

    The painterly, textured style gives the game a serious, weighty feel; the arena scene has good depth, and the camp map has a rich, detailed atmosphere that supports the 'solemn' mood. Concern: The lack of hard outlines and the muted, dark palette can make it difficult to quickly parse the positions of the player and enemies, especially in the busy camp map.

  6. Rain on Travertine — weakest 3; mean 3; other direction

    The airy, translucent washes create a unique and beautiful aesthetic; the arena is open and clear, and the camp map has a dreamlike quality that is visually distinct. Concern: The very light colors and lack of strong contours can make the game elements feel 'washed out' and less impactful, potentially reducing the clarity of the action.

  7. Salt & Scribe — weakest 3; mean 3; other direction

    The sketchy, nervous line work gives the game a raw, urgent feel; the arena scene is dynamic, and the camp map has a hand-drawn, personal quality. Concern: The 'broken' and 'nervous' lines can make the game feel unstable or unfinished; the low contrast and sparse detail can make it hard to read the camp map and distinguish different areas.

  8. The Vigil Cut — weakest 3; mean 3; other direction

    The heavy, carved lines and stark color palette create a powerful, defiant visual identity; the arena scene is dramatic and the camp map has a strong, imposing structure. Concern: The extreme density of the hatching and the harsh contrast can be visually overwhelming and fatiguing; the 'propagandistic' mood may not fit a game that requires clear, friendly readability.
