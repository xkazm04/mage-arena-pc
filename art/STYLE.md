[OWNER-CHOICE.md](OWNER-CHOICE.md). Its palette, broken umber contours and fresco
rendering are the baseline. Its A1 arena framing is superseded by the owner's

The immutable direction definitions are in `styles/a1-v1.json`. They distinguish
line, palette, shading, density and mood: Tessera & Lime; Salt & Scribe; Hearth
Under Guard; The Vigil Cut; Ash & Bronze; Forum in Four Inks; Ember After Dark;
Rain on Travertine. These are original hand-drawn digital **look studies**, generated
from authored prompts, not claims of manual painting or production readiness.

Every archived A1 direction uses the same arena base action and the same camp base
action, selected by `config-v1.json`; only the full style block changes. An explicit
correction appendix is allowed only against a hash-bound content rejection, is kept
in the attempt record, and is visible in the evidence manifest. The shared forward
absorb correction addresses a repeated spatial failure. Framing corrections on two
directions are separately recorded. No named artist, franchise or borrowed asset
is used as a style shortcut.

The current plan's season, Windows and mouse/keyboard decisions supersede the
baseline's loops, JVM and controller UI. Camp places and opening times are loaded
from the baseline `locations.csv`; slot vocabulary is read from `daySlots` in its
data. No reset, Echo or loop counter appears in the concepts. No stat gains or
combat quantities are asserted by the pictures. Generated maps provide atmosphere
and landmarks; labels and time slots are drawn in code. These are authored UI
studies, not gameplay screenshots.

Local models observe source semantics. Deterministic checks inspect source and
export bytes, aspect, size, alpha, basic contrast and provenance. Neither proves
an absorb's exact angle, casting timing, readable combat in motion or owner feel.
The generated outgoing bolt sometimes resembles a dart or is detached from the
hand. Residual defects belong in direct observations, not concealed by a pass label.

All rejected and superseded plates remain in `review/sources/` and the attempt
gallery. Current comparisons contain one arena and one camp per direction. Their
screens are derivatives with code-drawn UI; their untouched sources have independent
hashes. No material enters `assets/` or code packages in A1.
