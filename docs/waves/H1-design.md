# H1 - hit reactivity and persistent defeat

2026-10-04, before simulation/presentation implementation. D36/D37. Registry note read: `ai-registry/knowledge/game-production/systems-canon/realtime-combat-semantics/techniques/death-via-state-tag-not-input-disable.md`.

Replace the independent down boolean with a serialized DEFEATED state tag. One health/state authority atomically tags defeat, records its tick/direction, stops velocity/ward/roll, cancels paid commitments and emits the death event. Movement, cast entry/release, targeting, damage/status application, both AIs, projectile collision, encounter counts and HUD read the tag. Corpses stay in the actor array until bout reset. Already-launched projectiles and explicitly survivesOwner death hazards retain their authored lifetimes; corpses cannot receive them. Lab G is an explicit revival through the same authority; R creates a fresh bout.

Each positive HP delta, including ward chip, attempts a stagger. Roll i-frames and zero-damage/perfect contacts remain immune. Duration = clamp((floor + HP removed * slope) / effective poise, floor, ceiling), quantized to fixed ticks. Default floor 0.10 s, ceiling 0.20 s, slope 0.003 s/HP, tail 0.18 s after stun ends. Per-entity poise is authored (heavy enemies higher). During the tail hits still deal damage and flinch visually, but do not restart stun or cancel a fresh cast: the explicit anti-stunlock exception. A stagger clears velocity immediately, cancels pending mage casts/enemy tells, and keeps mana/cooldowns spent. Core tests cover player, Mage AI and roster enemies, guarded chip, fan hits, zero damage and roll.

Use A10 hit/death clips wherever available, own-entity fallback otherwise. Every damaging contact gets a short presentation-only recoil away from the recorded hit direction, squash/shake, white flash and number; the player also gets a screen-space cue. Death selects its clip once and holds the last frame. Missing death rotates and settles the current entity sprite to a lying ground pose. Aura fades. A separate presentation clock lets death settle even when the defeated bout's deterministic simulation is stopped; it never writes core state. Results leave the player's ground pose visible. Reduced motion removes shake/recoil, retaining clear hit/defeat cues.

The Lab Hit tab exposes simulation timings, poise scaling and presentation reaction parameters using paged six-row controls. Metrics count stagger and cast interruption separately. Version tuning exports and migrate old experiments explicitly. Save/load and replay serialize the tag, stagger/tail clocks and cast costs; dead snapshots remain corpses on reload.

Capture before/after fixed-seed duels (hits to defeat, TTK, stagger and cancelled casts), rerun the full 2,000 fights per wave census and document justified pacing bands without altering enemy HP to hide effects.
