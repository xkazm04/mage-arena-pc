# W3 — Water lines, branches, Flow and composition

Full wave card and design note, written before implementation on 2026-10-02. Depends on W2 (`2494ab9`); unlocks W4. Source reasoning: baseline `combat.json`, all rows of `spells-water.csv`, `spell-sheet-water.md`, `enemies.json` and `arena-tiers.json`. The first two Tiro waves reward ranged pressure and anti-pack areas; later mage bouts need counterplay to absorbing. This wave implements the Water kit and a preparation screen, not the camp or other schools.

## Data authority and reconciliations

The baseline CSV remains the sole authority for spell damage, timings, costs, geometry and effect amounts written there. The data compiler and pure catalog parser derive typed records and fail on unrecognized shapes. Supplemental values absent from the sheet (fan speed, wave width/speed, decoy targeting radius) live in the authored runtime JSON, with their purposes named. No copies of baseline values are hand-maintained. Combat owns Flow and collar rules; schools.json is descriptive and is not another runtime source.

- Bolt plus exactly three distinct Water lines. Slots 1–4 follow D4; the composition screen selects lines and branches before starting a fresh bout. No live loadout swaps. Branches exist only where CSV rows exist: Lash II, Mirror II, Orb IV. Empty branch columns mean no branch, not a missing spell. Borrowed schools await W8/camp integration.
- The CSV gives short cast times but longer threat telegraphs for several spells. A damaging/controlling effect resolves at `max(cast_s, telegraph_s)` after cast start. The commitment costs mana and starts the line cooldown immediately. Interrupts consume those costs. Each line has its own cooldown, shared across its tiers; tier unlock never clears cooldown.
- Positional linter means ground-targeted areas (Mire); direct cones, caster-centred rings, reaction-triggered Ripple and projectile impact bursts are not pre-positioned ground traps. Unblockables always satisfy combat's unblockable minimum. Their actual warning lasts at least that long, not merely their data label.
- Flow starts at zero: the first cast establishes a predecessor; five qualifying alternations reach five stacks on cast six, then **cast seven** can Crest. The sheet's fifth-cast claim is a prose defect. Rain Needle is the `bolt` line and can participate; staff is not a spell. The next accepted cast consumes a ready Crest, costs zero and uses the Crest damage multiplier; otherwise the updated stack count supplies the bonus. Repetition resets Flow, idle expiry is inclusive, and an unaffordable/on-cooldown/passive button does not change Flow. Perfect absorb adds a stack. Crest does not also multiply by the five-stack bonus.
- Mirror II passives remain unlocked while the line is slotted at later tiers; pressing a passive does not cast. Reflection only returns magic projectiles at or below the reflector's tier, with their original damage, toward the original source. Reflected projectiles are not reflected again (ordinary absorbs still work), avoiding infinite return loops. Ripple is a single activation and cannot recursively trigger another Ripple.
- Sheen multiplies actual perfect-refund entitlement; ordinary absorb has no base refund to multiply. Return Tide stores half the damage actually prevented by the ward, including reduced physical hits; its CSV cap applies to stored base damage before the normal Flow/Crest multiplier. It empties on accepted release even when no target is hit.
- Puddle slows occupants while active; Fog blocks line of sight and future AI target locks, while manual aim/projectiles still work. Freeze roots once after its warning. Tomb selects the nearest enemy to the cursor within caster range at commitment and encases it (immune, silent, immobile, resource reset) if still in range at resolution. Spring heals over fixed ticks; Font roots its caster during the windup. Mirage is an expiring target point for AI soft-lock; the player always has free mouse aim.

## Implementation

Add typed spell catalog, validated compositions and named presets (Undertow, Mirror tide, Rotation), per-line cooldowns, cast snapshots, zones/status effects, Flow and mirror hooks to the same kernel. Use activation-local hit sets for burst/ring deduplication; projectiles spawned by reactions are deferred until the next projectile pass. Damage, healing and statuses cannot affect Down actors. Wave reset clears effects, cooldowns, Flow and storage; no hidden resource refills during reports.

The preparation screen shows all five lines, tier paths, branch picks and the resulting three-button curve. In arena, slots show current names, cooldowns and costs; HUD shows Flow/Crest and next collar unlock. Telegraphs, zones and decoys use original placeholder geometry. Training remains available, with composition changes beginning a new bout.

## Tests and gates (commands from root)

1. `npm --prefix packages/core run build`; `npm --prefix packages/core test`: baseline W2 plus catalog completeness, missing/illegal branches, unique slots, all spell effects, timings, mana/cooldown failure atomicity, Flow/Crest, snapshot semantics across a tier unlock, area dedup, reflection tier/family restrictions, silence/immunity, status expiry and wave reset.
2. `npm --prefix packages/core run report:w3`: linter over every CSV row; duration/units and four-tier coverage; mutation probes for illegal branch and short telegraph; DPS and utility per 15-second collar window for each named composition against a magic dummy and an explicitly labelled stationary soldier formation. Soldier HP/counts come from the first Tiro spawn table. This is a **simulated stationary content probe**, not the W4 AI-fight census. Report clears/timeouts, mana, perfects, damage, healing and control; mana-limited actual damage is distinct from an authored unlimited-mana ceiling.
3. Dominance review compares damage across early/late windows together with healing, control, range and counter utility; raw DPS alone cannot reject a healing line. Flag a composition if it weakly wins every measured axis and strictly wins one; investigate any flag before commit. Do not disguise a zero-damage utility as DPS. W4 validates real enemy fights.
4. `npm --prefix packages/game run build`; `npm --prefix packages/game run smoke:w3`: production boot, choose presets/custom branches, start practice, select/cast slots with mouse and keyboard, observe a tier upgrade and capture preparation/arena screenshots in `docs/waves/W3-evidence/`. W2 regression smoke remains runnable.

Green build/tests and generated report required before the W3 status/log/one commit. Data changes, if necessary, must cite measured evidence and remain in their one authority. Kill: unexplained universal dominance or invalid/untelegraphed content means fix before advancing, not a claimed pass. Owner checks: composition clarity, in-place upgrades, Flow rhythm, whether each line earns its slot; **not felt** until the owner reports. W4 follows the commit.
