# A14.3 / session 10 / stage 2 / 2026-10-04

180/192. Cinder hound and Hush Moth have both hits, collapse and persistent
corpse in all four directions. Mire Maw has both hits in all directions and
front-pair collapse/corpse. Thornback has the front pair of all four states.

source slot are retained; rear scenes repeatedly turned toward the viewer or
supplied almost static standing keys. No third attempts or fake rear sprites.

rejected sheets. Session 10 totals 22 calls/images/charges, project 364. Every
creature call passed the original approved Covenant source image and its style
block, plus an A10/cropped identity reference. Front hits used a hash-bound crop
from that entity's gated front proof. No Grok call, refund, or provider reset
beyond the initially authorized agy recovery.

local magenta removal, whole-body scales, ground pivots, left mirroring and
exact final-key corpse reuse. Irregular grids use explicit crop rectangles or
authored component centres. Hush Moth front death honestly has four generated

small recoil on Mire Maw, and late-key scale variation are owner-review
findings. Thornback front hit heavy keys receive one uniform 0.84 scale factor
measured from repeated ready anatomy; limbs are never reshaped.

well as the A10 identity crop and within-sheet baseline. Direct hash-bound
facing observations supplement crop, silhouette, palette, scale and pivot
checks. Local vision may only reject or route. These checks do not approve
animation, anatomy or gameplay readability.
