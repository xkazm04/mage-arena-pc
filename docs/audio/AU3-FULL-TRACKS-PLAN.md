## The clock and the six-section form

Authored grid: **96 BPM, 4/4**, 0.625 s/beat, 2.5 s/bar, **six bars per 15 s cell**. Total: 60 bars / ten cells / 150 s. Dorian centres below are proposals, not measured keys of the kept recordings. Reject or edit a take that drifts off this requested grid before claiming adaptive readiness.

| Section | Time | Bars | Cells | Arrangement / potential tier material |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Entrance | 0–15 s | 1–6 | 1 | Dramatic first contact, restrained foundation; tier I |
| Theme statement | 15–45 s | 7–18 | 2–3 | Theme at 15, answered at 30; tier II then III density |
| Development | 45–75 s | 19–30 | 4–5 | First full-energy statement at 45, variation at 60; tier IV vocabulary |
| Tension | 75–105 s | 31–42 | 6–7 | Harmonic pressure, reduced then rebuilding orchestration; alternate tier III/IV material |
| Climax | 105–135 s | 43–54 | 8–9 | Strongest statement at 105, final answer at 120; tier IV |
| Release | 135–150 s | 55–60 | 10 | Theme fragment and deliberate ending, tail inside last bar; wave-end material |

Every internal 15 s boundary gets a clean downbeat and a complete preceding phrase; avoid long lead-in pickups across the edit. A section can span two cells but must still articulate its midpoint. Nominal tier unlocks at 0/15/30/45 s fit the opening progression. Later compositional tension/release is **not** a gameplay tier downgrade. Preserve the complete linear score as the principal asset; derive a separate adaptive edit/arrangement manifest.

Perfect absorbs advance the gameplay clock by 2 s, with at least 6 s between unlocks. Immediate collar/perfect feedback stays unquantized. Ramp an already-running sustained layer immediately; enter rhythmic material at the next verified beat (625 ms plus 50 ms scheduling horizon), or a compatible harmonic transition at the next bar (up to 2.55 s). Coalesce to the latest confirmed tier. Wave reset selects the entrance; pause freezes transport and gameplay. A long fight holds an approved interior tier-IV region or moves to another complete track, never repeats the dramatic entrance every fifteen seconds. A short fight uses a release edit. AU4 owns those mechanics.

## Adaptive deliverables and acceptance

Start with one approved full mix and non-overlapping intensity **edits** from its cells. Crossfade between compatible phrase entries; never stack two separately generated full mixes as if they were stems. For vertical layering, derive or author synchronized foundation, percussion, melody and pressure layers from the *same arrangement*, all with identical sample count/downbeat. Source separation may leak melody into the backing or blur attacks; if that happens use horizontal edits until real stems exist. The complete 150 s narrative remains available independently.

Proposed production gate (none claimed passed by an audition):

1. Owner hears the kept family and a memorable theme, identifies all six phases, and prefers the complete arc to repeating the sketch. Listen through twice, including one low-level run alongside representative combat cues. No repeated short block or accidental vocals. Score melodic recall, development, fatigue and gameplay space separately.
2. Decode the actual export; report duration, sample rate, sample count, channel count, peak and LUFS. Target PCM editing masters at 48 kHz; a decoded MP3 is a lossy-source PCM derivative, not an original lossless master. Keep provenance. Verify export entitlement before promising native PCM.
3. Mark the first true downbeat and all ten cell boundaries from the audio. Target 96 BPM, boundary error <=20 ms after permissible editing, no cumulative drift >20 ms over 150 s. If a musically good performance cannot meet this, use measured tempo in a non-grid playback mode or arrange it anew; do not falsely stamp 96 BPM into metadata.
4. Full tracks need a clean beginning and ending, **not** an end-to-start loop. Interior sustain edits need three consecutive repeats and every permitted transition: no click, missing downbeat, doubled hit, harmony clash, audible gap or conspicuous repeated fill. Numerical screen: boundary jump <=−60 dBFS and adjacent 0.5 s RMS difference <=3 dB after the intended crossfade. Those numbers flag candidates; listening still decides. Use 5–20 ms microfades for edits or one-bar equal-power overlaps when harmony permits; retain decay as a separate tail where needed. Never repair a musical hole by adding silence.
5. Raw source true peak <=−1 dBTP after mastering; working arena/title music around −26 LUFS, camp around −30 LUFS, whole representative game mix −20 LUFS ±2 and <=−1 dBTP. Preserve at least 6 dB summing headroom. Check the loudest permitted layer sum, every allowed subset, mono and small speakers; do not normalize every stem to full-mix loudness. Retain gain values and loudness logs in the manifest.
6. In AU4 replay wave start/reset, 15/30/45 s unlocks, accelerated perfects, multiple same-frame perfects, early wave end, long tier IV, pause/resume and speech ducking. Critical feedback cannot wait for music. D30 app gain/high-cut/compression/limiting/variation/cooldown policies remain runtime work. Record voice budget (32 total, music <=8 including crossfades) and audible masking failures.
