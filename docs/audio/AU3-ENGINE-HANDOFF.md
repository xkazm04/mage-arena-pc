# AU3 engine handoff

Load [manifest-au3.json](manifest-au3.json) from the same deployed asset tree as `docs/audio/`. Every `file`, `rawFile`, `sidecar`, section, sting and loop path is relative to the manifest URL, not the game page. Resolve with `new URL(assetPath, manifestUrl)`. No provider access, credentials or paid runtime dependency is required.

Two 150 s stereo masters are delivered: Reed oath and Lyre under iron, at 48 kHz / 7,200,000 PCM frames. They are lossy-source PCM derivatives, at -26 LUFS working level, with more than 11 dB true-peak headroom. Preserve their gain; two full mixes have a conservative summed-peak bound of -7.05 dBTP at unity music-bus gain. This is an arithmetic bound, not a complete combat mix test. D30 effects treatment, ducking and the game mix limiter remain AU4 work.

`linearPlaybackReady` means decoded and mastered, not owner approved. `productionApproved` is false and `ownerChoice` null. Play the score once from the entrance in a development preview; do not loop the whole score. Full-track approval and musical assessment are separate from technical file readiness.

`cells` gives ten exact 15 s ranges; `sectionMap` gives Entrance 0–15, Theme 15–45, Development 45–75, Tension 75–105, Climax 105–135, Release 135–150. All sample intervals are half-open `[startSample,endSample)`, relative to the 48 kHz **file** clock. WebAudio may resample on decoding: use `startSample / sampleRate` and durations in seconds for scheduling, rather than treating 48 kHz indices as device-buffer indices. Six section WAVs are bit-identical consecutive slices of the master; they are review/export convenience, not independent stems.

Proposed tier vocabulary: I cell 0; II cell 1; III cells 2 and 5; IV cells 3, 4, 6, 7, 8. Cell 9 is release. Sparse later sections do not lower the confirmed gameplay tier. `requestedBpm` is 96, while `verifiedBpm` and `downbeatOffsetSeconds` remain null. Onset screens support the clock, but no true-bar downbeat annotation exists. Consequently `adaptiveReady` is false and `allowedTransitions` is empty. Do not schedule beat/bar transitions from the estimator alone.

Each `sustainLoop` supplies a separate 15 s interior hold, its exact loop start/end, original source range, 2.5 s equal-power overlap, rotation and numerical three-repeat results. It is not the end of the full track. The rotation reduces sample discontinuity but is not a beat-aligned entry; approval/enabled are false until its phase, harmony, fills and join are heard. `releaseTail` separately indexes the non-looping natural source decay. Each `sting` is exactly 5 s: a release-theme fragment crossfaded into the original decay, with its edit recipe recorded. Its theme recognition and harmonic join also require review. Do not infer approval from file existence.

After musical approval, horizontal switching must preserve the collar contract: immediate critical tick/perfect feedback; coalesce pending requests to the latest confirmed tier; never delay gameplay for music. The nominal clock unlocks at 0/15/30/45 s; perfects advance it 2 s with a 6 s minimum gap. Use a verified beat/bar plus the 50 ms scheduling horizon for musical entries. Pause freezes transport/gameplay, wave reset selects the entrance, early end chooses an approved release, and long tier IV uses an approved interior sustain or another track. Limit these full mixes to two concurrent voices during a crossfade, within the bible's music <=8 / total <=32 budget. They must not be stacked as vertical layers.

Required remaining gate: two full listens including quiet combat cues, no vocals, kept-family melodic identity, development/contrast, real release, exact grid/downbeats, three sustain repeats, every allowed tier jump, mono/small speakers, pause/reset, accelerated and same-frame perfects, long/short fight, speech ducking, all permitted mixer subsets and the full cue-log mix. Until then this is a technical delivery for review, not a certified adaptive score.
