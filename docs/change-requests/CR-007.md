## What the port implements

Momentum is Air's resource, from `schools-combat.json` `air` only. Range `[0, 100]`. A non-air actor ignores Momentum, the drain multiplier, the roll multiplier, the evade, and the deflect, and carries no Air key.

Gain and decay:

- `perSecondMovingAbove4Mps` 20. The 4 m/s is the `4` in the key. Speed is this tick's locomotion, `|pos - previousPos|` over one step, read after the move and before the same tick's release. Walking (4.5 m/s), sprinting and rolling count. A Slipstream dash does not: it lands later in the tick and pays its own +30.
- `perSecondStill` 25. "Still" is zero displacement. Between 0 and 4 m/s, for example walking with the ward held (4.5 × 0.5 = 2.25 m/s), Momentum neither gains nor decays.
- Hit payments from `momentum_gain`: `N per hit`, `N per target` and `N per tick` pay the caster N on every hit its spell resolves, including a warded or perfected one. An evaded hit pays nothing. A deflected bolt pays the deflector nothing.

Thresholds apply when `momentum >= at`. A higher band replaces each effect it names. At 80 the 40 band's pierce and cheaper roll still apply.

- 40: `spellsPierce` 1 and `rollStaminaMult` 0.6. The pierce is one extra target. A projectile that connects, and is not perfected, flies on for one more body. Lines, beams and placed circles already hit every body in their shape, so the pierce changes nothing for them. The count is snapshotted at release. The roll multiplier applies to the roll cost and to the AI's "can I roll" check.
- 80: `spellRangeMult` 1.3. Applied at release to projectile flight range, line and beam length, and Downdraft's placed distance, and to the AI's range check. Not to the dash. `dash 6 m` is a distance, not a range.

`absorbDrainMult` 1.2 applies to every air actor's ward. Eye of the Storm's "Absorb drain x1.5" stacks on it, 1.2 × 1.5 = 1.8, while its lock lasts.

`perfectAbsorbExtra` is the deflect. On a perfect absorb, an air actor redirects a projectile of tier <= its unlocked tier along its own facing, which the kernel sets from the aim every step. The deflected bolt keeps speed, damage and tier. It flies `max(remaining, distance to the thrower + radius)`. It cannot be reflected or deflected again. For an air actor the deflect **replaces** the Water Mirror reflection its attached water preset would give. The final's preset, Mirror tide, has Mirror A. A bolt above the air actor's tier is perfected and stops, and nothing flies back.

## Spells

Ten rows from `spells-air.csv`, slots 0 through 9, in an Air catalogue beside the Water and Fire ones. Families: `UNBLOCKABLE` becomes `unblockable`, `absorbable` becomes `magic`, `n/a` stays apart on the three self and dash rows. A damage cell that starts with a digit parses as that number, so "12 per 0.25 s tick" is 12. Mana is spent on the press. Cooldown is `tick + ticks(cooldown_s)`. Windup is `max(cast_s, telegraph_s)` for every row: one windup, the Fire rule for every row but Sunfall. Cast time 0 releases on the press tick.

The sheet is read strictly. A header change, a numeric cell with anything but digits and a fraction, an unknown shape, damage, blockable or `momentum_gain` cell, a dash whose reach disagrees with `range_m`, a beam without a tick, a tick on a non-beam, a momentum mode that does not fit its shape, `n/a` on a damaging row, or Air Form notes without their two evade chances, their Momentum need and "unblockables are not evaded" are load failures.

## Gale mage (Lio)

With school rivals on, the Tiro final is Lio's air mage; the semifinal stays Brennic (Fire). The water composition stays attached, as CR-002 requires. Competence is the T07 model. Aim lead uses Gale Dart's 24 m/s.

`ChooseAirSpell`:

1. Tempest Lance from tier IV whenever it is legal and the target is not mid-roll. No mana banking, unlike Sunfall. No spell-choice draw.
2. Air Form when an aimed, blockable threat is seen (projectile, telegraph or windup), the form is down, and Momentum allows.
3. Slipstream while Momentum is under Air Form's 40, dashed along the current move, or sideways to the target when standing. On PC the dash end is kept inside the arena.
4. Otherwise the competence curve over the useful rows (damage above 0, or a zone; Slipstream and Air Form only by the rules above). Level 1 draws `mage <id> spell`. Level 2 and up sort Veering Bolt and Squall first, then damage × count, then the slot. Eye of the Storm, like Furnace Heart, is cast only when no useful row is legal.

Offerable means tier, cooldown by the next tick, mana, Air Form's need, and range × the 80 band. A dash and the self rows have no reach. A pending Air cast, a running volley, and a live Cyclone are threats to the opponent. A line or beam is a threat along its segment.

## PC readings beyond VR (not a change to the rules above)

- PC actors can be staggered (hit stun) and VR actors cannot. A staggered or encased air actor skips its move, so Momentum decays as still. A stagger is an interrupt, so it drops a volley or a Cyclone.
- The PC kernel does not read `combat.staffStrike.interruptsCasts`, so air.ts applies it to air casts: a connecting staff strike interrupts the pending cast and drops a volley or channel.
- Every PC interrupt (roll, stagger, encase, defeat, staff strike) reaches Air through the kernel's `SchoolRules.interrupted` hook, which drops the unthrown orbs and ends Cyclone. `SchoolRules.busy` keeps casts and the staff strike out while either runs. `SchoolRules.rooted` roots Tempest Lance's windup.
- Downdraft, Tempest Lance, Cyclone and Slipstream use the PC shared shapes (`shapes.ts`). Downdraft is queued with zero delay at release and lands the same tick, after the actor loop and before projectiles. It is marked as surviving its owner, because VR has already resolved it at release. Cyclone ticks in the same pass, along the live facing, with a fresh activation per tick. VR steps the channel inside the caster's turn; the outcome is the same, only the activation-id order across actors differs.
- Pace (difficulty) scales an air actor's windup above its warning floor, its cooldowns and its projectile speeds, as it does for Water. Parity runs have no pace.
- Air releases carry no cast recovery. Water's 0.05 s `castRecoveryS` is a PC feel value VR does not have.
- The PC overlay `school-balance.pc.json` may change Air cells and identity numbers. A parity run (`balance: 'canonical'`) ignores it. The 4 m/s speed comes from the key name, so the overlay cannot change it.
