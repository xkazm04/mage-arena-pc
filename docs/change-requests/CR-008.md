## What PC plays differently

### 1. The exchange-window rule (new; no canonical counterpart)

PC adds one close-duel rule, in `packages/core/src/arena/exchange.ts`. Its numbers are in `school-balance.pc.json`
under `exchange`. `vr:tuning-return` reads only the overlay's per-spell `overrides`, so it does not list these numbers;
they are written out here by hand:

| Key | PC value | VR value | Meaning |
|---|---|---|---|
| `exchange.closeRangeM` | 5 | none (rule absent) | owner-to-target distance at contact that makes a hit a close hit |
| `exchange.guardS` | 8 | none | how long the defender's window lasts after a clean close hit |
| `exchange.guardHits` | 8 | none | how many of that attacker's close blows the window's ward takes |
| `exchange.guardReduction` | 0.95 | none | share of each of those blows the ward stops (unblockable blows pass) |

The rule: a clean hit (damage through, no ward) delivered from 5 m or closer opens the defender's window against that
attacker. The attacker's next close blows land guarded, the way a held ward stops them, with no perfect absorb. A hit
from beyond 5 m or from another attacker is unaffected. PC opens a window only in a contact a Fire or Air actor takes
part in, and its parity runs (`balance: 'canonical'`) ignore the rule.

Evidence (PC, 200-seed builds census, `evidence/d1-exchange-before.json` -> `evidence/d1-exchange-after.json`):
- Fire close (Brennic's build): median 15.8 s -> 67.0 s, reference wins 47.0% -> 49.5%, full hits per exchange
  2.29 -> 0.61.
- Air close: 31.8 s -> 50.7 s, 52.5% -> 40.0%, 1.91 -> 1.30.
- Far builds hold. Air heavy: 73.9 s -> 75.8 s, 46.0% -> 46.5%.

**Why VR may want it.** VR's Fire mage brawls at the same 4-5 m. The dual-channel brief (W8 section 1, "PC tunes VR")
asks PC to return pacing findings as numbers. Two cautions:
- the numbers were tuned against PC's walking reference player, who chases into 5-8 m;
- a seated VR player fights at the dais distance, so `closeRangeM` should be re-measured on VR before it is adopted.
