# The Ledger

## The bets

- **Code decides the camp, the language model only speaks.** A deterministic social simulation (a utility planner over one shared menu of 14 activities, plus the owner's four archetype situations as state machines) decides every night. The model writes the Dawn Board, mostly at build time as a validated phrase bank; a live voice is optional. Zero runtime calls are needed.
- **The sister stack.** Fire TV + phone controller, Kotlin + libGDX, pure-JVM core. Wave 1 is a mage on the real Stick.
- **A curve, not a list.** Four slots of fixed spells, each dark until the collar unlocks its tier (15 s each, −2 s per perfect absorb). Composing a loadout is choosing how strong you are early versus late.
- **One season.** Six weeks, six Games, four endings computed from state.

## Why this loop holds

Every camp quantity has exactly one arena consequence: Vigor is HP, Focus is mana, Nerve is the absorb's economy, Guile is scouting, trust with a teacher is a spell, a feud is starting Heat. Every arena result writes back: gold, renown, the Trial, rumours, a rival's respect or a feud. Schemes touch the fight (poison is −20 stamina); a friend's warning foils them. The ending, the Breaking, is the parry mechanic played as trust.

## Biggest risk and the early test

The 0.18 s perfect absorb on a TV with a phone controller. W1 measures input age p95 on the Stick; W3 puts a perfect-absorb bot and the owner's hands on the same window, judged by the input's own timestamp with a 0.10 s grace. If the owner cannot land perfects, the gamepad becomes the default and the window is re-derived from data. A mechanical-feeling camp is tested in W2 by 1,000 headless seasons and the owner's read of the fact lists.

## Known limits

- The model does not decide outcomes. This is a deliberate departure from the owner's note; the Director of variant 2 can be added later behind the same schema.
- Every number is authored and unmeasured; feel is the owner's.
- Typed role-play (Plea) is deferred to an experiment after the slice.
- All names are invented here; renaming is a data change.
- No deaths in camp or below the Summa, by decision.
