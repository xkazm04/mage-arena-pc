# Mage Arena: the owner's concept notes (verbatim, 2026-10-01; typos left as written)

Name: Mage Arena
Concept: RPG about elemental mages from era of Roman empire, locked by romans into area where they train to arena battles between mages to make for their living.

Campaign starts with character pick from one of core elemental magic schools: Fire, Water, Earth, Air. All 4 characters are assigned to help romans in battle against barbarians serving as skippable tutorial. Once the battle is won, all mages are betrayed by romans, enprisoned and carried away into magical prison conceptually similar to Chronicles of Narnia - camp of mages is behind locked prison door and there is no escape from there.

The camp is divided into tents per magic school, each school is trying to cooperate and train together to increase their chances to succeed in arena events and earn gold for food and equipment. Each school members are specific with certain behavior characteristic. For example fire are hardheaded, aggressive and more powerful the more tension is made.

Goal of the campaign is balancing relationship between characters to learn skills, complete quests for equipment, earn chance to fight in arena to increase reputation.

## Mechanics

- Time based mechanic inspired by Persona series. User has multiple locations to visit, each to boost different area of stats or skills
- Arena does have multiple tiers based on character levels, school gives entrance to those best in their level (internal competition required). Each tier has multiple waves (from non-magical soldiers, creatures, at the end with other school mages in semifinal and final)
- Battle controller: Top level (diablo-like view) one hand always on movement, one to cast spells. Direction, mechanics of spells, cooldowns, casting time can vary. Parry mechanic should be there as 'magical absorb', spending mana for time holding the absorb. If perfect parry in specific window of impact, amount of mana comparable to spell level is recovered, timer to unlock next spell level shorten (some spells can be unblockable)
- Battle resources: Mana with regeneration, Time - each time period (15s) new tier of spells can be used with higher impact, player starts with lightweight magic arrow spells, as character grows and time goes in arena the player gets into more sophisticated creations, Stamina - For melee and physical escape mechanics (sprint, roll). Spell slots are limited and player is responsible to compose unlocked tiers before each arena event.
- LLM based reconcilliation - All characters in camp do have different written characteristics and daily reconcilliation retarget their goals, motivations and activities each day. All characters have same scale of options each day yet different starting position - reconcilliation process calculates what each character did, how it evolved
- To keep reconcilliation and story sensible, we need to design multiple archetype situations. For example:
a) There is a group of mages without tent, outcasts without resources trying to survive. Everyone is against them
b) Schools are trying to damage each other before arena event via quests (poison, persuade to doubt schools, steal). Result is givin loyalty points to plus and minus. Quests can be composed as decision text game (D&D style), can be predefined but also part of reconcilliation.
c) Duality of tribalism - characters from player's school are rivals leading into befriend and cooperate with same level of other schools, schools are competing against each other
d) Bond of main 4 characters - characters from start share event of enprisonment, this leads into more frequent reaching each other, trying to cooperate, plot how they can achieve better positions in their own schools, learning about the world and way to escape
- Extreme experiment of the game would be to allow in certain situation roleplaying and typing custom message.
- Magic can't be used inside camp, as the magical prison blocks it. Camp is guarded my arcane mage guardians which cooperate with romans, preventing violance and misbehavior
- Arena is outside the prison and it offers a way to get away if 2 finalists of highest arena tier cooperate and decide to destroy the arena (end of the game)

## Main characters
- Fire character lusts blood, lives as warrior to become the strongest mage in hiearchy and eliminate any foe
- Water character is female, extremely intelligent and knowledgable about spells, using intriques and strategy to eliminate foes before arena
- Earth character is a protector, powerful and calm presence, working towards goal to find way out from the arena and return to his family
- Air chatacter's motivation is anger towards roman and his trapping in this world, he tries to eliminate those playing by roman rules and hierarchy, trying to create new tent, persuading others to become new force in the world and revolt once it dominates
